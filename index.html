Research

Topology meets scalable computing

Persistent homology at scale

Distributed and piecewise computation of persistent homology, and compact boundary matrices that make large filtrations practical.

Sparsified and polytopal complexes

Constructing smaller topological spaces, including β-sparsified hypergraph-based simplicial complexes and polytopal complexes, that preserve the structure that matters.

Geometry and benchmarks

Resource-efficient Delaunay triangulation in higher dimensions, and test-data generators for evaluating topological data analysis pipelines.
